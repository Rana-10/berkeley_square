# Design language of UK property finance / bridging lenders and private banks

Method note: pages were fetched via WebFetch, which returns text converted to markdown and summarised by a small model. NO CSS, fonts, colours or pixel layout were available. Anything about typography, colour, imagery style or layout is labelled "(inferred)" and is not verified. LendInvest returned 403; Arbuthnot Latham was not fetched.

## 1. How do they present loan products and key terms (rate, LTV, term)?

### Takeaway
Practice varies: Cohort puts the key terms (amount, term, LTV) on each transaction rather than in a product table; Octane shows "rate from" per product; Ortus gives only a 6-24 month term; Pure lists product families as icon cards. None of the fetched homepages used a full rate/LTV/term table, so a ruled definition list or table is an under-used pattern.

### Cited Findings
- Cohort Capital describes bridging for acquisitions, refinancing, planning/conversion and sale periods; typical borrowing £1m-£100m; average turnaround just under two weeks. Each transaction carries amount, term and LTV (NoMad Hotel Covent Garden £73m/24 months/70%; Life Science Park Sunbury £11m/12 months/65%; Chilham Castle Kent £9.1m/18 months/60%) — [Cohort Capital](https://cohortcapital.com)
- Octane Capital: four product tiles with a "from" rate: Buy to Let 6.59% pa fixed; Bridging 0.73% pm; Refurbishment 0.73% pm; Developer exit 0.69% pm. No LTV or term on the homepage — [Octane Capital](https://www.octanecapital.co.uk)
- Ortus Secured Finance: Residential (Residential Bridging, High-Value Single Unit, Development Exit) and Commercial (Commercial Bridging, Stabilisation Loan, Commercial Mortgage); terms 6 to 24 months; rates "competitive" with no figures; no LTV — [Ortus Secured Finance](https://www.ortussecuredfinance.co.uk)
- Hampshire Trust Bank: audience routing (Personal / Business / Intermediaries); specialist property finance in tabs (Development, Specialist Mortgages, Bridging); savings shown as "up to" AER tiles; broker platform PUMA and "Find your BDM" links — [HTB](https://www.htb.co.uk/)
- Pure Property Finance (a broker): 6-7 product cards, each with icon, italic subheading, short paragraph, "Discover more"; mega-menu by Bridging/Development/Commercial/Residential/Secured/Insurance; vocabulary includes LGDV, LTC, mezzanine, senior debt — [Pure Property Finance](https://www.purepropertyfinance.co.uk)
- Together: three audience tiles (Commercial, Personal, Intermediaries), "Let's get started" CTA and a selector ("I need finance for myself" / "I'm acting on behalf of a client") — [Together](https://www.togethermoney.com)
- Coutts: three service pillars (Wealth Management, Private Banking, Commercial Banking), each with a short headline and one "Explore" link; little product detail on the homepage — [Coutts](https://www.coutts.com)

### Inferences
- Lenders put quantified terms on case studies and keep products qualitative; a ruled definition list or table "Amount | Term | LTV | Rate" per product is an under-used, credible pattern.
- Audience routing (borrower vs broker) is a recurring primary navigation device.
- Rates as "from" figures with a "pm"/"pa" basis (Octane) are conventional shorthand and should carry an indicative qualifier.

### Gaps
- Product sub-pages were not fetched, so no real rate/LTV tables were seen.
- LendInvest (403) not covered.

## 2. How are process steps, stats and case studies laid out?

### Takeaway
Stats appear as big-number counters with small labels (Cohort); case studies as three-up items with amount, term, LTV and a link; process is thin or a short enquiry-to-completion timeline.

### Cited Findings
- Cohort stats: counter blocks for loans originated, loans completed (£1bn), average closing time (days) and average LTV (%); each a large number with a small label — [Cohort Capital](https://cohortcapital.com)
- Cohort process: a short enquiry-to-completion timeline; hero line "Clear. Simple. Decisive." — [Cohort Capital](https://cohortcapital.com)
- Cohort case studies: three cards with heading, amount, term, LTV, arrow icon, short summary, "Learn More"; "View all transactions" link — [Cohort Capital](https://cohortcapital.com)
- Ortus: credit-backed agreement in principle within 24 hours; dated case studies (Nov 2024, Oct 2024), e.g. a Grade II-listed manor house, nearly £3m; established 2013; offices London, Manchester, Glasgow, Belfast — [Ortus](https://www.ortussecuredfinance.co.uk)
- Pure: trust strip (Google 4.9 stars from 123 reviews, Planning Portal, GoCompare logos), awards, press logos (This is Money, Guardian, FT, i, Sunday Times), two case studies; its lender count is inconsistent (100+, over 60, "unrestricted number") — [Pure Property Finance](https://www.purepropertyfinance.co.uk)
- Together: heritage claim "Funding property ambitions since 1974" / "over 50 years" — [Together](https://www.togethermoney.com)
- Octane: no process, stats or LTV on homepage; "Apply now" to portal — [Octane Capital](https://www.octanecapital.co.uk)
- HTB: award badges (Feefo Platinum Trusted Service, 2026 awards) stand in for stats — [HTB](https://www.htb.co.uk/)

### Inferences
- A numbered process timeline is rare in this sample, so it can differentiate; 4-5 steps with timings.
- Case-study rows work best with consistent data (amount, term, LTV, asset type, location, date).
- Avoid inconsistent numbers (Pure's lender count); consistency is a trust signal.

### Gaps
- Case-study page layouts and the exact visual treatment (row vs card) are not verifiable from text.

## 3. What imagery do they use?

### Takeaway
Property-led photography for lenders; lifestyle imagery for private banking. Visual treatment is inferred only.

### Cited Findings
- Cohort: property photography, background image behind contact form, testimonial headshots with five-star graphics (from image labels) — [Cohort Capital](https://cohortcapital.com)
- Octane: rotating property image carousel in hero — [Octane Capital](https://www.octanecapital.co.uk)
- HTB: people-focused photography plus trust/award badges — [HTB](https://www.htb.co.uk/)
- Coutts: lifestyle scenes (gardens, kitchen, coffee, pool, networking, family, artwork), not product shots — [Coutts](https://www.coutts.com)
- Cohort's named assets (NoMad Hotel Covent Garden, Chilham Castle Kent, Sunbury life science park) suggest prime London, heritage and commercial assets — [Cohort Capital](https://cohortcapital.com)

### Inferences
- (inferred) Real photography of named UK assets (stucco townhouses, listed houses, construction, skylines), one per case-study row, suits the brief better than stock people.
- (inferred) Carousels are common among competitors; a static editorial hero reads more private-bank.

### Gaps
- Colour grade, aspect ratios and crops not available.

## 4. How do they show forms, calculators and enquiry CTAs?

### Takeaway
Short repeated verb CTAs ("Loan Enquiry", "Apply now", "Get a free quote", "Let's get started"); forms in a closing contact band; Together offers calculators.

### Cited Findings
- Cohort: "Loan Enquiry" in nav and hero plus "Get in touch"; closing "Have a question?" block with a form over a background image — [Cohort Capital](https://cohortcapital.com)
- Octane: "Apply now", "Email now", phone number — [Octane Capital](https://www.octanecapital.co.uk)
- Pure: "Speak to an advisor", "Get a free quote", callback — [Pure Property Finance](https://www.purepropertyfinance.co.uk)
- Together: personalised borrowing calculators, EPC Hub, "Help with your application" — [Together](https://www.togethermoney.com)
- Coutts: "Contact us", "Join", help centre in footer; sparse CTAs — [Coutts](https://www.coutts.com)

### Inferences
- A private-bank register would use one quiet, repeated "Make an enquiry" plus a named contact and telephone, not several buttons per section.
- (inferred) A short form with loan amount, security location, purpose and timescale suits the sector.

### Gaps
- Form fields and any refined calculator examples were not retrievable.

## 5. Typography, colour, footers, and regulatory/disclaimer conventions

### Takeaway
Footers carry dense small-print regulatory text whose wording depends on authorisation status. Typography and colour could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- Cohort Capital Ltd "is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority"; bridging activities "do not constitute regulated investment business"; clients "will not be eligible for compensation under the rules of the Financial Services Compensation Scheme". Footer: Park Lane address, LinkedIn/email icons, copyright, Privacy and Cookie links — [Cohort Capital](https://cohortcapital.com)
- Octane: "Octane Capital is a trading name of Aldermore Bank PLC"; PRA-authorised, FCA/PRA-regulated; notes some lending (buy-to-let, commercial lending to limited companies) is not regulated; asset finance may be exempt under the FSMA Regulated Activities Order 2001 — [Octane Capital](https://www.octanecapital.co.uk)
- HTB: company number, registered office 80 Fenchurch Street, "Authorised by the PRA and regulated by the PRA and FCA", FRN 204601; subsidiary FRN 165116; FSCS and Feefo badges — [HTB](https://www.htb.co.uk/)
- Pure (broker): repossession warning, FCA does not regulate some BTL/overseas/commercial finance, trading name of Pure Commercial Finance Ltd (FCA 744421), credit broker not lender, commission disclosure, ICO registration, NACFB Assured — [Pure Property Finance](https://www.purepropertyfinance.co.uk)
- Together: trading style of several companies; Together Personal Finance FCA 305253; Blemain FCA 719121; FLA member; Crystal Mark; CCA logo — [Together](https://www.togethermoney.com)
- Coutts: small-print block (registration, registered office, PRA/FCA status, firm reference number), FSCS and B Corp marks, footer columns Help and support / About / Further information — [Coutts](https://www.coutts.com)
- Ortus Secured Finance footer: address, privacy and GDPR documents, copyright 2022, no regulatory statement found on the page — [Ortus Secured Finance](https://www.ortussecuredfinance.co.uk)
- Tone/headings (summariser-reported, low confidence): Pure uses sentence-case headings with one italic keyword; Ortus lowercase headings ("why ortus?"); Together a lowercase wordmark — respective sites above.

### Inferences
- (inferred, not verified) Editorial look: serif display plus clean sans body, restrained palette (ink/navy, stone off-white, one deep accent), hairline rules, generous margins.
- Pattern for an unregulated bridging lender: state not FCA-authorised, not regulated investment business, no FSCS; company name/number and registered office; privacy/cookies. For a broker add NACFB and commission disclosure. Match the client's real status and get compliance review.

### Gaps
- Fonts, hex colours, spacing: no CSS access.
- Arbuthnot Latham not fetched; Coutts text summarised, not quoted.

### Takeaway

### Cited Findings
